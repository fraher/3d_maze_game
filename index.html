Overtaken

For a thousand years the kingdom of Eldenmoor knew peace — until the night the sky tore open.

Down from the wound between the stars came the alien horde. They overran the royal castle in a single night and dragged the King, the Queen, and their children into its depths.

You are Sir Aldric, the last knight left standing. You take up your blade and breach the castle gate. Eleven floors rise above you, each crawling with invaders from beyond the stars.

Climb. Find the stairs. Free the royal family. And discover what the horde truly crossed the void to claim — for something ancient sleeps beneath these stones, and the aliens mean to wake it.

Move: W A S D / Arrows · Attack: Space (or the on-screen button) · Walk onto glowing stairs to climb

You Have Fallen

Your blade slips from your grasp. The alien horde closes over you, and the cold stone of the castle rises to meet you.

Somewhere above, the royal family waits in the dark for a rescue that will not come — not today.

But Eldenmoor remembers its knights. From the ruin of the realm another will rise, take up the sword, and climb where you fell.

The siege is not yet lost.

The Kingdom Saved

The great dragon shudders and falls, its plasma blood hissing across the cold stone.

From the cells below rise familiar voices — the King, the Queen, the children — alive. Eldenmoor is saved.

But as the beast dies, its eye fixes on you, and a final truth burns into your mind: the invaders never came to conquer. They came for the Starheart, the relic your ancestors swore to guard, buried far beneath the castle.

You sheathe your sword. For now, the kingdom is whole again — yet the dragon's dying whisper lingers: “Others will come for what sleeps below…”
